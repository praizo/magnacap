at 2400px wide, then JPEG-compressed locally. Next.js supplies responsive

The Careers photo was re-downloaded at 4000 × 2670 without the CDN's automatic
compression, then cropped to 2000 × 2500 and saved as a quality-90 WebP. Matching
the desktop panel's 4:5 shape avoids enlarging a short landscape image to fill it.

The Advantage section now uses five dedicated WebP photos matched to each benefit.
Execution, discretion, reputation and insight are prepared at 1800 × 1350, from
sources at least 2400px wide, with quality 92. Price discovery is a native-resolution
992 × 790 crop of the trading-screen photo, saved at quality 94 without upscaling.
All five are served directly to avoid another compression pass. Desktop uses a
4:3 frame; mobile uses 16:10 with the report-review photo anchored to the top.

Art direction: preserve the existing full-width homepage photograph, slate scrim,
and rounded inner-page media panels. Use real workplace photography featuring
Black people, with a formal business discussion for the homepage, collaboration
for team/service pages, an analyst portrait for insights, and a headset portrait
for contact. LILY retains its product screenshots.

Image selection rule: stock photography must predominantly feature Black people.
All-Black groups and mixed Black and white groups are welcome; never use photos
featuring only white people. This applies to alternate and unused stock assets
as well as images currently displayed on the site. Unused stock photos and the
unused LILY mark were removed on 29 October 2026.
